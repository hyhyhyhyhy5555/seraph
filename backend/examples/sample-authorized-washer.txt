SW-2000 Authorized Washer Manual

Page 1
The SW-2000 washer supports normal wash, quick wash, extra rinse, and spin-only mode.
Close the door, choose a course, and press start.

Page 2
If water does not enter the washer, check the faucet, inlet hose, and inlet filter.
If the hose is bent or the filter is blocked, water supply can fail.
